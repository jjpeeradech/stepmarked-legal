StepMarked is a travel journal: take a short quiz to meet your travel twin, then mark the places, food, and countries you have visited.

1. Contact

Email kuptipong.park@gmail.com with any question or problem. We usually reply within a few days.

2. Delete your account

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, travel history, persona results, and badge progress, and cannot be undone. If you signed in with Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is also revoked.

You can also email us to request deletion.

3. What does "Claim My Profile" do?

You can use StepMarked without signing in. Your journey is then tied to this device only, and is lost if you delete the app or change phones.

Claiming your profile links it to Sign in with Apple, Google, or an email and password, so you can sign in again on any device and keep your journey.

4. Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and continue, then choose "Forgot password?". We email you a one-time code to set a new password.
